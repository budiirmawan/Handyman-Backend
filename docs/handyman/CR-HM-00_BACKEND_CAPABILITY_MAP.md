| Capability | Existing Module/Path | Decision | Existing Authority | Handyman Extension | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tenant Service Request | `src/modules/tenant-service-requests` | EXTEND | Existing tenant service-request capability. | Extend for the Handyman customer request lifecycle. | Handyman request lifecycle and states remain distinct; do not absorb FM workflows. |
| Service Catalog | `src/modules/service-catalog` | EXTEND | Existing service catalog capability. | Extend for Handyman services and variants, with future material discovery model: Service → Service Variant → Common Material Profile → Material/SKU → Image → Specification/Compatibility → Reference Price → Typical Quantity → Commonality → Customer Material Option. | Evidence | `src/modules/evidence` | EXTEND | Existing evidence capability. | Extend for Handyman BEFORE/DURING/AFTER/QC/DEFECT/RECTIFICATION/MATERIAL/BAST/WARRANTY evidence. | Evidence requirements and lifecycle remain Handyman-context; do not absorb FM workflows. |
| Inventory/Material | `src/modules/material-requests`; `src/modules/inventory-items`; `src/modules/inventory-material-reservations`; `src/modules/inventory-stock-movements`; `src/modules/inventory-work-order-material-usages`; `src/modules/purchase-requests`; `src/modules/purchase-orders` | EXTEND | Existing material requests, inventory items, reservations, stock movement, usage, and purchasing capabilities. | Extend the Handyman material lifecycle: Estimated → Approved → Issued/Purchased → Used → Returned → Final Usage. | Handyman material states and reconciliation remain distinct; do not absorb FM inventory or procurement workflows. ## Backend New Capabilities

| Capability | Decision | Responsibility | Reuses | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Secure BM Super App Handoff | NEW | Accept trusted customer/building/unit identity from BM Super App and establish the Handyman session/context. | `src/modules/tenant-building-contexts`; `src/modules/buildings` for context only. | Handoff establishes identity/context; it is not a replacement for backend authorization. Across these NEW capabilities, existing Asentra engines may be reused as infrastructure, but Handyman-specific business semantics are owned here. |
| Immutable Channel Attribution | NEW | Preserve originating BM/building/channel attribution from request through the transaction lifecycle. | `src/modules/tenant-service-requests`; `src/modules/work-orders` as lifecycle infrastructure. | Attribution is immutable and survives request → transaction; it must not be silently overwritten or inferred later. Handyman owns its attribution semantics. |
| Handyman Work Crew | NEW | Model Lead Worker/PIC and helpers; track crew assignment, replacement, and history; helper login is not required. | `src/modules/workforce`; `src/modules/work-order-assignments` for assignment infrastructure. | Handyman owns crew composition, responsibility, and history semantics; existing workforce/assignment engines are infrastructure only. |
| Handyman Work Session | NEW | Record CHECK-IN, START WORK, PAUSE, RESUME, MATERIAL RUN, COMPLETE, and CHECK-OUT; separately track presence, work, and billable time. | `src/modules/work-orders`; `src/modules/schedules`; `src/modules/evidence`; `src/modules/audit` as infrastructure. | Presence Time != Actual Work Time != Billable Time. Handyman owns session events and time semantics; FM task/session workflows are not adopted. |
| Handyman Pricing Execution | NEW | Execute Handyman pricing modes HOURLY, FIXED_SCOPE, INSPECTION_FIRST, and VISIT_FEE, including crew pricing modes. | `src/modules/service-catalog`; `src/modules/price-catalog-entries` as catalog/reference-price inputs only; `src/modules/work-orders` as work context. | Catalog/reference prices are inputs only, not final transaction charges. Handyman owns pricing execution and charge calculation; do not place it in price-catalog authority. |
| Commercial Agreement & BM Fee Rules | NEW | Maintain versioned commercial agreements and configurable fee basis; a default/reference model may use LABOR_ONLY. | `src/modules/price-catalog-entries` for reference inputs; `src/modules/audit` for shared audit infrastructure. | Handyman owns agreement versions and fee rules. No transaction calculation inside SaaS subscription logic; SaaS Billing != Handyman Customer Transaction Ledger. |
| Handyman Customer Transaction Ledger | NEW | Own customer charge lines, payment allocation, refund, reversal, adjustment, and immutable financial history. | `src/modules/work-orders`; `src/modules/evidence`; `src/modules/integration-outbox`; `src/modules/audit` as infrastructure only. | SaaS Billing != Handyman Customer Transaction Ledger. The ledger owns Handyman customer transaction history; existing engines do not become its financial authority. |
| Provider & BM Entitlement | NEW | Derive provider earning and BM fee earning from governed Handyman transaction and commercial rules. | `src/modules/vendors` for provider identity; Handyman Customer Transaction Ledger and Commercial Agreement & BM Fee Rules for governed inputs. | SaaS Product Entitlement != Handyman Provider/BM Financial Entitlement. This is financial entitlement, not SaaS product access or subscription entitlement. |
| Settlement & Reconciliation | NEW | Track EARNED, PAYABLE, INCLUDED_IN_SETTLEMENT, and SETTLED states, including REVERSED / ADJUSTED / DISPUTED exceptions. | Provider & BM Entitlement and Handyman Customer Transaction Ledger for Handyman-owned financial inputs; `src/modules/audit`; `src/modules/integration-outbox` as infrastructure. | Handyman owns settlement and reconciliation states and exceptions; do not infer them from SaaS billing or subscription state. |
| Handyman Service Warranty & Claim | NEW | Own workmanship warranty, material warranty, warranty claims, free warranty rework, and chargeable additional work. | `src/modules/evidence`; `src/modules/checklist-templates`; `src/modules/findings`; `src/modules/finding-rework` as evidence/QC/finding infrastructure. | Asset Warranty != Handyman Service Warranty. Handyman service warranty and claim semantics are separate from asset warranty semantics; warranty rework and additional charges follow Handyman rules. |
