# BM commission 02, part 01 — gross-to-net ledger

`postHandymanBmCommissionLedger(actor, { clientId, entitlementId, determination, idempotencyKey, correctionFactId? })` is an internal scoped Finance command. The current actor must have `handyman.finance.read`, `handyman.finance.manage`, and access to the earned entitlement's Building. The only accepted tax input is the existing independently approved case pin; party, amounts, tax rules, tax dates and rounding are never supplied by the posting caller. `readHandymanBmCommissionLedger` returns immutable entries to a scoped Finance reader.

An original entry revalidates the uncorrected EARNED entitlement, historical BM/Building binding, current 0500 approval and both existing verified calculators under source and approval locks. An already-final ADD/REDUCE variation, any unlinked customer ledger correction/refund/reversal, a stale or unsupported case, or a future tax point blocks posting. A future point is a forecast, not proof that the event occurred.

The correction bridge supports its existing single linked 0414/0417 decrease or reversal, with its own current 0502 maker/checker tax approval and original 0500 baseline. This ledger additionally requires the matching **original commission ledger entry posted before the correction**. The original insert and subsequent source mutations serialize on the customer transaction; no caller timestamp is used to manufacture that ordering. Corrections predating the original ledger entry lack this witness and are blocked, even if a read-only corrected projection exists. Additional, unlinked or unsupported corrections remain blocked.

Each row stores the full verified projection and source/approval checksums, exact beneficiary and Building, approval and fact IDs, and both target totals and signed changes. The original row's changes equal its totals. A correction's changes equal corrected totals minus its immutable predecessor; sum **deltas**, not target totals, to obtain the last posted position. A later unlinked source change can make that history stale; reading it is not a fresh authorization. A retry with the same scoped idempotency key and intent returns the original row; a different intent or duplicate fact conflicts. Source writers, final variation admission, tax windows and the posting transaction are serialized before revalidation. Append-only and rollback guards preserve history.

The PPh field is a **withholding calculation**, not evidence that withholding occurred. The entry explicitly does not assert withholding, remittance or payment. It does not transition PAYABLE/SETTLED, authorize settlement or payout, issue an invoice or tax document, or alter entitlements, prior decisions or settlement records. External evidence references remain subject to Finance's independent review; this calculation does not certify their legal content.
