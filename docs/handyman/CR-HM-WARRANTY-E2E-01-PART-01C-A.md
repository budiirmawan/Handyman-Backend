# Warranty claim assessment — PART 01C-A

## Authority and scope

`handyman.warranty.assess` is a dedicated permission, registered but **not granted to any role by default**. A reviewer and decision-maker must be active, explicitly granted that permission, assigned to the exact Client/Building, and different from the authenticated claim opener and submitter. A Tenant PIC of that tenant cannot act as assessor even with a grant. Care intake and submission still use the existing `tenant_company.manage` route and Building wall; that permission alone does not decide coverage.

New claims pin the existing issued certificate at OPEN. The input must contain `certificateId`, `certificateSourceSha256`, `certificatePdfSha256`, `certificateManifestVersion`, `itemLineId` (the completed root quotation line), and `coverageKind` (`LABOR` or `PARTS`). The certificate has no separate mutable version number: its immutable document number and source/PDF digests identify the issued document, while the manifest version identifies the accepted completion basis. The certificate must be current, intact, and for the claim's warranty, BAST, Client, Building and scope. Only explicitly agreed certificate item terms apply; the legacy automatic BOTH coverage is not authority. Missing, unlisted, not-performed, NOT_APPLICABLE, mismatched or ambiguous items fail closed.

The claim's actual filing time is generated by PostgreSQL when SUBMIT is written, not supplied by the caller. It must be in the item's `[startsAt, endsAtExclusive)` interval at microsecond precision. Lifecycle `expired_at` is not contractual expiry. The submitted time, submitter and claim evidence are frozen. An incomplete evidence record can be submitted so a reviewer can say `NEEDS_EVIDENCE`; it cannot support a covered or rejected decision until finalized, nonempty and byte-verified.

## Assessment and decision

`POST /api/v1/handyman/service-warranty-claims/:claimId/assessments` takes all six certificate/item pins above, `expectedRevision` (0 for the first assessment), `result` (`COVERED`, `REJECTED`, `NEEDS_EVIDENCE`), `reason`, `conditionsDisposition` (`SATISFIED`, `NOT_SATISFIED`, `UNDETERMINED`), `conditionsReason`, `exclusionsDisposition` (`NOT_APPLICABLE`, `APPLIES`, `UNDETERMINED`), `exclusionsReason`, `evidenceRecordId`, and `idempotencyKey`.

The assessor explicitly records the interpretation and supporting reasons. The server does not parse free-text contractual exclusions, infer that they do not apply, or treat legacy generic coverage as proof. COVERED requires the selected coverage kind, actual submission within the contractual period, explicit `SATISFIED`/`NOT_APPLICABLE` findings, and finalized evidence whose immutable file metadata matches the managed bytes. REJECTED likewise requires finalized byte-verified evidence. NEEDS_EVIDENCE records the actual incomplete evidence and leaves the claim submitted, without implying that coverage has been established. Assessments and their evidence snapshots are immutable; a new assessment uses the next revision. A stale version or competing revision is rejected; an identical retry replays its original event.

`POST .../:claimId/approve` and `POST .../:claimId/reject` require the current `assessmentId` plus `idempotencyKey` (and `decisionNote` for rejection). The latest assessment must respectively be `COVERED` or `REJECTED`; the server rechecks the certificate, actual filing period, evidence bytes, assessor authority, and exact assessment pin. A different-key duplicate decision is refused. No rework execution, QC, financial or PDF issuance behavior is implemented here.

`GET .../:claimId/assessments` returns the pinned item/terms, immutable assessment history and `coveredApproval` only when the persisted approved decision and its sources remain verifiable. It requires either the dedicated assessor permission or `tenant_company.read`, plus exact-Building access. `readCoveredWarrantyClaimApproval` is the equivalent internal projection for a later rework decision.

Historical claims and certificates are retained without invented consent or retroactive eligibility. Existing unbound drafts/submissions cannot be submitted or decided as newly certified claims; an unbound submitted claim can still be withdrawn under the existing lifecycle. Previously approved claims without a verified binding are not silently upgraded and cannot create or newly authorize warranty rework. Existing already-authorized historical rework is not rewritten. Closed-scope rework execution and fresh QC remain outside this part.
