## Confirmed gaps / bounded integration

- Existing quotation/catalog/pricing, approved ADD/REDUCE, customer payment/decision/allocation, correction-netted ledger, percentage entitlement, settlement, invoice reconciliation and Manager FINAL CLOSE engines are reused. PR60's 8 categories/24 services and FIXED/ESTIMATED/INSPECTION_FIRST gates are unchanged. No replacement financial, quotation, approval, catalog or payment engine was introduced.
- The missing persisted facts were: a preissue inspection offer and previsit agreement, once-only inspection credit linkage, explicit effective-dated Indonesian tax facts, actual bank receipt reconciliation, actual external fiscal references, certified noncash withholding linkage and the BM fiscal basis. Migration **0482** adds eight append-only tables and bounded uniqueness/draft-only/credited-funds guards. The original financial histories remain authoritative.
- Inspection offers/agreements are immutable and require actual inspection-required triage. Paid fees reuse a separately approved, same-customer/Client/Building/space single-LABOR fee scope using an appropriate existing service; no main-job inspection-first gate is bypassed. FREE has no fee scope/payment/credit. A direct no-inspection job cannot acquire an inspection fee. Paid treatment and agreed non-proceed disposition must be shown before the visit; no cancellation/no-show charge or automatic refund is invented.
- PAID_CREDITED supports the explicitly agreed **separate inspection service + job discount** arrangement. Full unrefunded **Finance-confirmed and bank-reconciled** fee funds are required. The existing line-scoped correction posts one job discount, never copies a payment or allocates the same money twice. Replays bind actor/target/key; terms can be spent once. Refund/reversal after consumption requires explicit compensation and is blocked. Transaction-before-terms lock order also protects the concurrent credit/refund boundary.
- Manager can hold the existing `handyman.finance.manage`/`handyman.finance.read` capabilities on the same account; no separate Finance persona/application is mandatory. Role names alone are not permission. Care reports/uploads proof only and cannot confirm/reject funds or attest actual bank receipt. Payment `availableActions` now also require explicit Finance authority instead of advertising verifier actions to Care.
- New authenticated thin routes live in the existing Finance family. Companion reads do not mutate quotation DTOs. Accountless Care sees only bounded previsit/issued-version financial terms through its **existing selected-request `/projection`**, using the same single SQL statement, live workspace/occupancy scope and no-store behavior. The original request-detail safe projection is unchanged. No User/PIC borrowing, enrollment, customer login, taxpayer/bank-reference exposure or new Care money authority was added.
- Tax reconciliation distinguishes contractual/posted gross, service net, DPP, PPN, income tax due, withholding, self-remittance liability, actual cash and once-only noncash certificate credit. `cashLedger` is the existing published money read, not a second balance engine; null/no posted transaction is not PAID. Reviewed IDR gross-inclusive ordinary PMK131 Art.3 and explicit non-PKP are supported; facts are never inferred from the word Handyman or a catalog category.
- Advance/installment references use actual Finance-reconciled receipt time, not Care reporting/confirmation time, and the full approved commercial scope even when only some ledger lines exist. Approved variations need exact-version equivalent reviewed tax facts. Later documents link their exact predecessor; allocation/replay does not create VAT. FINAL uses current QC and canonical qualified BAST, not raw ACCEPTED/digest history. Refunded advances, unknown adjustments, stale/missing facts and negative certified-withholding compensation block readiness.
- Known fiscal obligations gate actual invoice **FINALIZE**, not draft line composition. Certified withholding uses one linked existing ledger correction, not invented cash. Global issuer/document and payer/certificate uniqueness, normalized bounded references and actor-safe replay prevent reuse. Legacy unprofiled commercial invoices are retained for compatibility and are **not** tax invoices/fiscal certification.
- BM uses the existing exact agreement percentage/basis and entitlement/settlement engine. The governed adapter restores certified noncash withholding to revenue and excludes VAT from the applicable labor basis; it does not introduce a universal percentage/base. BM has its own tax facts, actual fiscal/withholding/cash reference and Handyman payer identity. Governed settlement refuses missing/stale fiscal amounts and corrected claims. Accrued/payable/settled history remains separate from tenant invoice and WO close; a positive test settles BM while the scope is still AUTHORIZED.

## Explicit unsupported / external/config blockers

1. **No live issuer/receiver integrations:** genuine bank reconciliation, current provider/BM taxpayer facts, classification/qualification, payer/exemption evidence, external fiscal/eBupot references and canonical identifiers must be supplied/reviewed by authorized operators. Self-remittance is a liability read, not proof of government deposit. No DJP issuance/approval/SPT signing/submission, live bank API or production tax certification was added.
2. **Bounded fiscal capability, not universal coverage:** tax-exclusive, non-IDR tax FX, special PMK11/exempt/luxury regimes, mixed/changed tax classifications, unsupported payer/recipient taxes (including an unreviewed individual-provider PPh21 case), monthly/late/legacy fiscal documents and true job-advance inspection reclassification are blocked. Do not silently select the ordinary regime to bypass this.
3. **Compensation is not automated:** spent inspection-credit refund/reversal, cancellation/replacement of a certified withholding credit, refunded advance fiscal compensation and governed BM fiscal replacement after correction require a separately reviewed forward compensation capability. Current history remains intact and readiness/settlement stays blocked; a new external reference alone is not a repair. Ordinary existing monetary corrections/reversals and explicit non-PKP no-proceed handling remain available within their original bounds.
4. **Contract/identity setup:** supply the actual applicable BM agreement percentage and supported labor basis/`AFTER_FULL_CUSTOMER_COLLECTION` timing; other bases/timing are not defaulted into this funded-settlement engine. Grant existing Finance/read/settlement capabilities to the actual Handyman Manager as applicable, not blanket authority to a generic BM Manager or Care. Set up the appropriate existing separately approved inspection-fee service/scope; no new universal fee seed was installed.
5. **Live accountless consent still external:** the independent BM commercial-decision producer/reviewed scoped enrollment remains required. Care statements, proof uploads, membership, scheduling consent and financial terms reads are not customer quotation/BAST consent. No borrowed PIC/User or new purpose promotion.
6. **Operations:** 0482 was applied only to disposable databases. No Railway/Biznet/production DB/deployment/PR was touched. Deploying code would require the normal separately authorized migration process first. Preserve append-only fiscal/linkage history; the destructive structural down/up test is not a production rollback plan once financial facts exist—use governed roll-forward recovery.
