## 2. Governance Rules & Architectural Firewalls (FROZEN)

All Customer Care backend transport/projection work for `CR-HM-17` (`Handyman-Frontend` journey integration) is bound by the following non-negotiable rules:

1. **Expose projections and thin transports only:** Backend additions are strictly limited to read-only projections over existing domain tables/contracts and thin HTTP/OpenAPI wrappers over existing in-process services.
2. **Do not duplicate lifecycle authority:** Every state machine, transition guard, idempotency boundary, and domain invariant remains owned exclusively by its certified authority (`CR-HM-01..16`). No parallel tables, shadow statuses, or second write paths may be introduced.
3. **Customer Care acts via BM Super App:** Customer Care operator provenance enters exclusively via the certified `CR-HM-01 Amendment 01` signed handoff assertion (`actor.type = 'CUSTOMER_CARE'`, dedicated capability-enabled integration, server-resolved `care_actor_id` in `handyman_handoff_care_actors` → single-use exchange → immutable `handyman_channel_attributions` actor columns). No local `users` row or session is fabricated for the BM operator (`D4–D8`).
4. **Represented tenant / building / unit remains Backend-resolved:** Customer (`tenantCompanyId`, optional `tenantPicId`) and location (`clientId`, `buildingId`, `floorId`, `areaId`, `roomId`, `spaceId`) are always resolved server-side from the `CR-HM-01` attribution, `CR-HM-02` request snapshot, or `CR-HM-06` execution-scope snapshot. Caller-supplied context/identity fields in bodies or queries are never trusted as authority.
5. **No worker / provider / BM settlement authority leakage:**
   - Field-execution mutations in `CR-HM-07` (arrival evaluation), `CR-HM-08` (work session check-in/start/pause/material-run/resume/complete/check-out), `CR-HM-09` (material estimate/approve/issue/purchase/use/return/settle), and `CR-HM-10` (evidence create/add/finalize, QC open/item/finish, defect transitions) remain **strictly Crew-Lead-only** (`resolveHandymanAssignmentLead`). Customer Care receives **read-only** visibility over those field execution states.
   - `CR-HM-14` (`handyman-financial-entitlements`, `handyman-settlement`, `handyman-financial-read`) remains backend-internal (`CR-HM-14_PART05_HTTP_GATE_DECISION.md`). Zero provider entitlement, BM fee entitlement, payout, settlement batch, or reconciliation state may leak into any Customer Care endpoint.
6. **Payment customer view != provider / BM settlement:** Customer Care financial visibility (`B6`) binds exclusively to `CR-HM-13` (`handyman-customer-ledger-read`, `handyman-customer-payments`, `handyman-customer-transactions`) — customer charge lines (`LABOR` and `MATERIAL` separated), customer payments, payment allocations, and customer ledger corrections (`REFUND`, `REVERSAL`, `ADJUSTMENT`). It never reads or exposes `CR-HM-14` provider/BM settlement or `CR-HM-12` internal BM fee beneficiary/entitlement derivation.
7. **No FM / SaaS fallback:** Zero fallback, join, FK, or dual-write to FM modules (`tenant_service_requests`, `work_requests`, `work_orders`, `permits`, `bast_documents`, `tenant_invoices`, `vendor_quotations`, `vendor_invoices`, `asset_warranties`, `work-order-sla-register`) or SaaS control-plane modules (`platform_*`, `subscriptions`, `module_entitlements`).
8. **Preserve existing permissions and audit boundaries:**
   - Customer Care HTTP reads use `authenticationMiddleware` + `requirePermission('tenant_company.read')` + server-side `contextAccessService.canAccessClient(req.auth.userId, clientId)` (and building scope where applicable).
   - Customer Care HTTP commands (where thin transport wraps existing customer/operator commands) use `authenticationMiddleware` + `requirePermission('tenant_company.manage')` + `contextAccessService.canAccessClient` + existing single-use idempotency keys and `recordOperationalEvent` / `recordHandymanEvent` correlation.
