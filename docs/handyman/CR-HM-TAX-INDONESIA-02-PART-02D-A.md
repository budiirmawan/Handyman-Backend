# CR-HM-TAX-INDONESIA-02 — Part 02D-A: BM commission correction lineage

## Contract and authority

`readHandymanBmCommissionCorrectionLineage(actor, { clientId, entitlementId })` is an internal Finance read. It accepts no caller-supplied party, amount, event or tax input. An active user with `handyman.finance.read` and access to the original Building is required. It re-resolves the immutable EARNED BM fact, Client, request, transaction, Building, agreement/version, original labor basis, amount and beneficiary at the original posting instant. The public read uses one PostgreSQL `REPEATABLE READ READ ONLY` transaction; its internal snapshot entry point rejects autocommit, read-write and weaker isolation. The response has a versioned schema and deterministic SHA-256 checksum; repeating the read against unchanged sources yields the same result. It is not a financial posting, an authorization, or a promise that a later transaction sees the same state.

The original verified-party check is shared with the tax source reader but is now explicitly an **EARNED anchor**, not a claim that the current ledger still matches it. Original source checksums for unchanged tax sources remain V1. A legacy Client-organization fact remains that beneficiary kind; if it predates verifiable Building coverage, the immutable Client/definition and agreement anchors are returned with `LEGACY_COVERAGE_UNRESOLVED`, no substituted legal counterparty, and an unresolved blocker. A missing or inconsistent distinct-party binding fails closed.

## Events, dimensions, and provenance

- Completed ADD/REDUCE variations are admitted only for the original Client/request/scope/Building, with matching quotation thread/version, approved tenant decision, recorded verified evidence, authorized operations, execution, guarded latest Supervisor QC PASS, and a verified accepted BAST. The finding, optional proposal, review, revised quotation, decision, authorization, execution, QC and final-eligibility audit events must form a complete, ordered, consistent chain. Draft, rejected, incomplete and unverified rows are not correction events; a purported final row with missing evidence or inconsistent context instead creates an unresolved blocker. The evidence check consumes the persisted VERIFIED upload record; it does not re-hash storage bytes or newly adjudicate an old decision.
- `authorizedQuoteDelta` is the signed **quoted** amount, not a posted charge or BM commission change. For ADD, a quotation line's own revised version can suggest a charge candidate; the composition anchor may still name the original transaction version (0458). For REDUCE, the original target line can suggest adjustment candidates. Neither relationship proves the variation caused that financial fact. `financialLink` remains `UNPROVEN`, `commissionImpact` remains null, and candidates are not counted a second time as variation impacts.
- Each valid posted charge candidate appears once with its actual quotation line/version, composition-anchor version, basis ID, posting event ID, line kind and signed posted amount. Its relationship to the earning-time basis is `UNKNOWN`.
- Ledger correction facts appear once per 0414 ID, with the recorded kind, source IDs, actor, reason, idempotency key, time, currency and read-contract version. REFUND, payment REVERSAL, allocation REVERSAL and governed manual ADJUSTMENT are different dimensions: respectively customer refund, payment-record reversal, allocation unapplication, and charge reduction. They are **not additive across dimensions**. Payment-sourced events require one matching confirmation event and return its ID. A source that is absent, cross-transaction, mismatched, pending, rejected or ambiguously confirmed is excluded as an authoritative ledger event and reported as an unresolved blocker. Equal repeated corrections on the same source are flagged as possible duplicates, not assumed to be either duplicates or legitimate installments.
- The 0417 entitlement-correction cause is not a foreign key. A recorded BM correction is nested under its one same-Client, same-transaction ledger cause, never emitted as a second independent economic event. Its signed **recorded** commission amount is surfaced only when the cause is a validated post-earning line-scoped LABOR adjustment, the direction/chronology and bounded recorded chain are consistent, the original beneficiary binding is verified, and no possible duplicate or earlier unresolved fact compromises it. A reversal must match the remaining recorded fact balance; an unresolved or simultaneous-order chain cannot be silently continued. This is a consistency check over existing facts, not a corrected commission or tax computation. Missing, foreign, refund-linked, material-only, ambiguous, or inconsistent causes retain IDs and reasons but no signed commission impact. In particular, a gross refund or even an allocation to a labor line cannot by itself prove a BM commission delta.

The response supplies no aggregate correction amount, corrected earning, net commission, credit, payable or settlement result. `correctedCommission` is always null and `correctedTaxCalculation` is `UNSUPPORTED`. Economic source amounts in different dimensions, a quote, a candidate posted charge and an entitlement fact must never be summed together.

## Historical baseline and tax boundary

The EARNED table did not persist the complete set of charge and correction IDs included at derivation. Transaction-start timestamps do not prove commit order, and matching current totals do not prove identical constituents. Therefore every unlinked ledger fact and variation has an unknown relation to the earning baseline, and changed current totals are only a warning, never a reconstructed baseline. All ambiguous, possible duplicate and unmatched sources remain unresolved; equal totals, timestamps, quoted prices or shared lines do not establish causation.

The original 02C tax case and settlement history are never rewritten. The existing tax-source reader continues to reject recorded entitlement corrections and changed ledger totals. It now also conservatively rejects a transaction containing *any* ledger correction or a final-eligibility variation, even when totals still match or the correction may have preceded earning: the missing baseline prevents safely distinguishing those cases. Existing approved cases stay intact but fresh PPN/PPh or consolidated projections fail closed. A later governed part must define proof of baseline, recomputation, credit and tax treatment before corrected calculations are supported. No rates, tax rounding, invoices, credit notes, settlement state, payout, PDF, UI, migration or unrelated Finance behavior are introduced here.
