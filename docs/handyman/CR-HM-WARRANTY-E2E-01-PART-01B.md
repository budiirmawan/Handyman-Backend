# Warranty certificate — PART 01B

Prospective issuance only; no legacy documents, consent, item periods, or signatures are synthesized. An existing lifecycle warranty must be anchored to the exact personally accepted BAST and its immutable official PDF. The current Lead completion facts are rebuilt against the accepted manifest, independent QC verification and authorized scope under the scope lock. Historic BASTs without that evidence remain ineligible. Claim/rework states and Work Request closure do not themselves end contractual coverage.

## Agreement and periods

For original items, the approved quotation decision must pin the exact issued source and PDF with the explicit warranty-terms protocol. For ADDs the exact issued, tenant-approved and Operations-authorized variation line is required. Only explicit `warrantyChanges` and immutable supersession edges can amend a term: each predecessor document, decision, hashes and terms must match. An incidental line in a later quotation is not an amendment. Missing, ambiguous or legacy sources are not assumed to be covered. Completed/partial items with positive actual quantity are eligible only when their final agreed coverage is `LABOR`, `PARTS` or `BOTH`; `NONE`, `NOT_PERFORMED` and zero work are excluded. At least one eligible item is required. Units are frozen from the official BAST, not mutable master data.

The actual BAST acceptance timestamp is the start. `DURATION_DAYS` is precisely consecutive 24-hour days from that instant. A `FIXED_END_DATE_JAKARTA` covers its entire stated local date through midnight at the start of the following date in Asia/Jakarta. The PDF prints exact UTC start and **exclusive** UTC end per item, retaining PostgreSQL microseconds. An end at or before acceptance is ineligible. The lifecycle `expired_at` is an EXPIRE event, **not** the agreed future expiry, and is never used in the calculation. Each PDF explicitly states the agreed terms and limits without presenting a signature or inferring that a recorded document guarantees an active claim.

## Issue and manual delivery

`POST /api/v1/handyman/execution-scopes/:scopeId/warranty-certificates` requires authenticated, active exact-Building commercial Operations authority (including the existing `handyman.commercial.manage` and `tenant_company.manage` gates). Body: `warrantyId`, `bastId`, `manifestId`, integer `manifestVersion`, lowercase `manifestSha256`, `idempotencyKey`, and optional `documentLanguage` (`id`/`en`). Issue stores its number, immutable source, approved offer and BAST snapshots, manifest, lineage, exact terms, PDF bytes and SHA-256 atomically. Replaying the same actor/key/pins returns the same artifact; another key or source cannot issue a duplicate for the lifecycle warranty. Durable primary PostgreSQL and active issuer/settings are required. Sequence gaps on rollback are normal.

Active Building Care can manually download `/api/v1/handyman/execution-scopes/:scopeId/warranty-certificates/:certificateId.pdf`. A currently granted Care workspace actor can download `/api/v1/handyman/care/requests/:requestId/warranty-certificates/:certificateId/document.pdf` with the same exact `propertyId`, `tenantCompanyId`, `buildingId` and optional `spaceId` represented-occupancy selection used by the BAST/Invoice endpoints. Both return private/no-store immutable bytes and source/content SHA-256 headers. No delivery, tenant signature, or new consent is recorded by a download.

Current delivery fails closed if the accepted manifest is superseded, work sessions/proofs change or are revoked, QC is no longer the same PASS, a new authorized variation appears, or warranty supersession edges change. The exact original remains immutable. Local authorized Building Care may retrieve it only via the explicitly labeled `/warranty-certificates/:certificateId/historical.pdf` archive (historical filename and header); workspace current-delivery cannot retrieve a stale copy. Since the existing lifecycle permits only one warranty for a scope/BAST, a changed accepted basis cannot silently replace or reissue the certificate: obtaining new authoritative BAST/completion and a governed replacement workflow is outside this part. Contractual expiry alone does not change an archival document, and ordinary claim/rework lifecycle status is not a reason to discard it.
