# CR-HM-PAYMENT-E2E-01 — payment evidence and official invoice allocation

The official inclusive Handyman invoice remains an immutable billing artifact. It is not a payment or a generic `tenant_invoices` row. The existing customer payment ledger, payment-proof evidence, Finance confirmation, bank reconciliation, charge-line allocations, corrections and on-demand tenant payment receipt remain the money/document authorities.

## Report, verify, allocate

* An authenticated tenant may use the existing scope `customer-payments` reporting endpoint, and an exact-Building Care user may use the existing Care request payment endpoint. DP, TERMIN and PELUNASAN reports enter the ledger as `PENDING`; Care projects that as `PENDING_VERIFICATION`. A post-issuance report requires a nonempty payment reference. The reported amount, payer and currency come from the existing payment and execution scope, not from the invoice PDF.
* The existing Care workspace invoice/payment proof endpoint accepts PHOTO or DOCUMENT evidence only for the exact represented occupancy, request, invoice, payment and pending claim. A workspace Care actor remains a Care actor, not a fabricated local user. Finance may retrieve the same hashed payment-bound evidence through the existing Finance proof read endpoints.
* Finance can independently report an observed bank transfer without customer evidence: `POST /handyman/execution-scopes/:scope/invoices/:invoice/payments/observed` with `amount`, `channel: BANK_TRANSFER`, `obligationStage`, `externalReference`, `idempotencyKey` and optional neutral provider fields. This creates only a pending claim, not an assertion that funds were verified. Replaying a key with a different amount or reference fails.
* A Manager/Finance user with `handyman.finance.manage` and exact-Building access must explicitly use the existing confirm/reject command. Finance may separately record actual bank receipt time and bank reference with the existing reconciliation command. No upload, invoice, report, notification or allocation fabricates bank confirmation; no automatic receipt is issued.
* Finance explicitly allocates confirmed money through the existing payment-allocation endpoint. The exact official invoice/payment link and frozen invoice charge line are required; payment, line, currency and net REDUCE caps still apply. Allocations are immutable and idempotent. Reversals and refunds are independent forward-only ledger facts.

## Binding and balance

Payments recorded after official issuance are linked to that invoice without verifying or allocating them. Payment linking and official invoice allocation use the ledger/source fence and READ COMMITTED, so a stale repeatable-read writer cannot silently miss an invoice or concurrent allocation. For a payment that predates issuance, Finance must explicitly `POST /handyman/execution-scopes/:scope/invoices/:invoice/payments/:payment/link`; repeating the command returns the same link. It cannot change the payment amount or reference. Existing allocations are recognized once after linking; a missing/ambiguous reference, wrong scope, or allocation that does not fit the invoice's frozen net line requires an independent reconciliation/correction. A pre-invoice payment without a reported reference can only be linked after a real Finance bank reconciliation supplies one.

References are normalized for case and surrounding whitespace and reserved per Client across reported payment and reconciled bank references, including across execution scopes. Historical duplicates are not reinterpreted: they are blocked from a new link until independently corrected. Reservations and invoice/payment links are immutable. Each invoice/payment join rechecks Client, Building, request, scope, tenant payer, currency and transaction; a payment is linked to at most one official invoice.

`GET /handyman/execution-scopes/:scope/invoices/:invoice/payment-status` requires `handyman.finance.read` and the exact Building. Care's existing scoped request projection exposes the same balance. The projection uses only linked `CONFIRMED` payments and non-reversed allocations, net of payment refunds. It separately exposes verified-but-unallocated funds, excess funds, and unlinked verified amounts/allocations. `RECONCILIATION_REQUIRED` and `EXCESS_FUNDS` are explicit; excess is never silently treated as full settlement. If correction history is inconsistent, status fails closed. Invoice amount/PDF do not change. For refunds that leave allocations in place, a governed allocation reversal/correction may be needed before reallocating that line.

## Receipts

The existing Finance on-demand receipt command remains one immutable artifact per payment and requires its existing independent verification rule (independent confirmation, or independent reconciliation if the reporter self-confirmed). A newly issued receipt for a linked official invoice includes the exact official invoice ID and number. An earlier immutable receipt issued before the invoice keeps its original snapshot and is never retroactively relabeled. Receipt access still uses the original Finance or exact Care occupancy authorization.

No gateway, automatic bank synchronization, automatic verification, receipt issuance, refund, settlement or invoice PDF rewrite is introduced.
