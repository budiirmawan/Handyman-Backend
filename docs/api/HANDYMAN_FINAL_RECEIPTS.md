# Official Handyman Final Receipt

`POST /api/v1/handyman/execution-scopes/{scopeId}/invoices/{invoiceId}/final-receipt`
issues an immutable numbered PDF on demand. An active user with
`handyman.finance.manage` and exact Building access is required; the body and
query must be empty. A replay returns the original document. The document is a
snapshot of settlement, not another payment, and records the actual issuer,
settlement date, invoice and its approved ADD/REDUCE, verified allocations, and
the identifiers/checksums of existing independently issued Payment Receipts.
No Payment Receipt is generated by this operation.

The issuer uses the official invoice's frozen identity and source. Finance must
explicitly link independently confirmed DP/termin payments made before the
invoice to that exact invoice. Every live allocation requires its own verified
payment and existing official Payment Receipt. Issuance fails closed on a
pending payment, remaining balance, unlinked funds, duplicate/overallocated
funds, or a ledger adjustment outside the invoice's approved REDUCE sources.
The existing REFUND fact has no separate outbound settlement verification;
therefore **any refund requires independent governed reconciliation and blocks
issuance**, rather than claiming that money has been returned. No refund or
ledger history is rewritten. Later financial facts remain permitted and do not
rewrite the historical, as-of receipt.

`GET /api/v1/handyman/execution-scopes/{scopeId}/invoices/{invoiceId}/final-receipt.pdf`
requires scoped Finance read or `handyman.care.building`; the authenticated Care
workspace also supports `GET /api/v1/handyman/care/requests/{requestId}/invoices/{invoiceId}/final-receipt.pdf`
with its existing property, Building, tenant and space selection. Downloads are
private/no-store, verify source/PDF checksums and do not mark a document sent or
delivered.
