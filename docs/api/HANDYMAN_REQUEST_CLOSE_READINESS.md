# Handyman Request close readiness

`GET /api/v1/handyman/requests/{handymanRequestId}/close-readiness` returns a live, read-only `READY` or `BLOCKED` assessment for the **whole Request**. The authenticated actor must be active, have access to the exact Client/Building, meet the existing Operations scope-set authority (`tenant_company.manage` and `handyman.commercial.manage`), and have `handyman.finance.read` and `handyman.customer_tax.read`. No query parameters are accepted; the response is `Cache-Control: private, no-store`.

The assessment re-reads established ORIGINAL scopes and their current verified completion, guarded QC and independently accepted BAST. Every scope must have one exact official invoice whose current approved commercial source, ADD/REDUCE, corrections and verified tax classification still match the immutable invoice. Missing or ambiguous invoice/scope links block; an invoice for one scope cannot cover another. Each invoice must have independently verified, fully linked and allocated payments, a Finance-issued Payment Receipt for every allocated payment, and no remaining balance, unlinked funds, excess funds or pending payment. Refund corrections block because independent outbound refund settlement is not established.

Each invoice also needs its exact issued Final Receipt, verified against current reconciliation and the immutable invoice. Its delivery journal must contain an authenticated recipient PIC `DELIVERED` acknowledgment of the exact SENT event and receipt/version/checksums. A Care-reported `SENT` or a download is not acknowledgment. Historical PIC consent and delivery are checked against their recorded authority, not reassigned to today's PIC.

For each completed item, the accepted quotation or explicitly consented variation/amendment must establish its warranty terms. Explicit contractual `NONE` is `NOT_APPLICABLE`; absence of a certificate is never treated as NONE. Covered items require an integrity-verified, current issued Warranty Certificate covering the exact accepted BAST, completion and agreed items. Expiration of a contractual warranty period does not erase the certificate or decide a claim.

The response includes `operationalReadiness`, per-scope invoice/receipt/delivery/warranty facts, and `blockers` with `code`, `message`, `executionScopeId` and `invoiceId` (IDs are null where not applicable). Examples include `OFFICIAL_INVOICE_MISSING`, `INVOICE_LINKAGE_AMBIGUOUS`, `INVOICE_SCOPE_UNIDENTIFIED`, `INVOICE_COVERAGE_UNVERIFIED`, `INVOICE_APPROVAL_UNVERIFIED`, `FINANCIAL_BALANCE_UNRESOLVED`, `PAYMENT_VERIFICATION_PENDING`, `PAYMENT_RECEIPT_MISSING`, `REFUND_UNRESOLVED`, `FINAL_RECEIPT_MISSING`, `FINAL_RECEIPT_STALE`, `FINAL_RECEIPT_DELIVERY_UNACKNOWLEDGED`, `WARRANTY_CERTIFICATE_MISSING` and the existing scope-completion/BAST codes. Conflicting or unverifiable historical documents are blocked, not reconstructed.

`observedAt` describes this read; the scope-set revision/fingerprint cover the scope-set projection only, **not** Finance, receipt delivery or warranty changes. READY is not a persistent close authorization. The Main Request CLOSE command independently re-evaluates and fences these facts in its own transaction; see [the CLOSE contract](HANDYMAN_REQUEST_CLOSE.md). This endpoint never closes a Request or scope, changes Finance or warranty history, generates documents, depends on legacy tenant invoices, or treats unpaid BM commission settlement as a tenant close blocker.
