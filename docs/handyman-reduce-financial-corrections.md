# Authorized REDUCE financial binding

`POST /api/v1/handyman/execution-scopes/:scopeId/variations/:variationId/financial-correction` requires `handyman.finance.manage`, current access to the exact Building, and `{ "expectedVersion": 5, "idempotencyKey": "..." }`. The version is the current authorized variation version, not a caller-supplied price. Finance may post after Tenant approval and Operations authorization; this does not create QC, BAST acceptance, an invoice, or evidence of payment.

The approved original quotation line must already have its own immutable charge equal to the original quoted line amount. A usage-composed partial charge fails closed for separate reconciliation rather than risking a second deduction. The command verifies the pinned Tenant decision, Operations authorization, exact Client/Building, original and revised quotation versions, quantity, currency and signed amount. It atomically records one ordinary line-scoped `ADJUSTMENT` using the existing positive-magnitude ledger contract and one immutable provenance binding containing the negative signed delta. The binding is **not** an additional ledger entry and must not be summed again. A zero-price REDUCE records only a zero binding, not a fabricated zero-valued ledger adjustment. Current billable values come from the existing correction-netted ledger read.

Replays converge on the same binding; another key or actor cannot repost it. Historical unbound adjustments on the target line or transaction are ambiguous and block posting rather than being guessed or adopted. A later generic adjustment on the same line or transaction must have a distinct, independently linked financial source; the existing inspection and fiscal credit bindings remain independent. Missing original charges, stale approval, overruns, finalized invoices and mismatched context fail closed. A new invoice cannot be finalized while an authorized REDUCE lacks its binding; this ordering guard does not certify tax or alter invoice calculations. The new guards do not backfill past variations or alter ADD, original charges, receipts, paid allocations, entitlement or settlement facts.

An already-paid reduced line can become overpaid: existing allocations and receipts remain historical facts, and the ledger exposes a negative outstanding balance. New allocations cannot exceed the corrected line, and allocation summaries on REDUCE ledgers use net charge and reversal facts; any refund, allocation reversal, entitlement correction or settlement exception remains a separate governed Finance action. This feature does not address the separate tax-exclusive invoice blocker.
